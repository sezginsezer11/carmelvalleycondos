## Life at The Heights

Built in 2003, The Heights is a gated community on Carmel Country Road with tile roofs, stucco exteriors and mature palms. Most homes are recorded as condominiums in the MLS, even though the community is often called a townhome community, and they range from 1-bedroom starter homes to 3-bedrooms of more than 1,400 sq ft. See the full [The Heights community profile](https://www.carmelvalley.com/condos-for-sale/the-heights/).

### Why buyers choose The Heights

- **Gated and quiet.** A controlled-access entrance and well-kept grounds give it a private, settled feel.
- **Walk to school and errands.** Carmel Valley Middle School is about 5 minutes on foot, and Del Mar Highlands Town Center about 11.
- **Room to grow.** With 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom homes, you can move up within the community instead of leaving it.

## Is The Heights the Right Community for You?

Who it suits best, and the honest tradeoffs to weigh before you buy.

### A great fit for

- **First-Time Buyers** — 1-bedrooms of about 770–800 sq ft in a gated 92130 community, a few minutes' walk from the middle school.
- **Growing Families** — 2- and 3-bedroom homes with Carmel Valley Middle School down the street and Torrey Pines High under a mile away.
- **Downsizers** — Gated, HOA-managed grounds with a pool, spa and clubhouse, close to shops, the library and the recreation center.

### Tradeoffs to weigh

- **Mello-Roos on top of property tax** — Most listings show about $389–$675 a year. Ask for the exact amount on a unit's tax bill.
- **Condo ownership** — Most homes are recorded as condominiums, so check the floor plan and what the association insures before you buy.
- **Elementary assignment varies in listings** — Most show Carmel Del Mar Elementary, some Carmel Creek. Confirm with Del Mar Union for the specific address.
- **Few sales a year** — About 6 homes a year have sold here, so the right size may take time to come up.

## The Heights FAQs

### What do homes at The Heights sell for?

Recent sales at The Heights ranged from about $585,000 to $1,079,000. By size: 1-bedrooms $585K–$640K (median $610K; 4 sales in the last 12 months), 2-bedrooms $800K–$939K (median $848K; 3 sales in the last 12 months) and 3-bedrooms $790K–$1.1M (median $915K; 4 sales in the last 24 months). Condition, floor level and location in the community explain most of the gap. Ask for a current comparison before you make an offer.

### What are the monthly dues at The Heights?

MLS listings from 2025 and 2026 show $392–$485 a month, varying by unit. The fee covers the gated entrance, pool, spa, fitness center, clubhouse, landscaping and exterior maintenance. Confirm the current fee for a specific unit.

### Does The Heights have Mello-Roos?

Yes. Most recent listings show a Mello-Roos (Community Facilities District) assessment of about $389–$675 a year on top of the regular property tax. Ask for the exact figure on a unit's tax bill.

### Are homes at The Heights townhomes or condos?

The community is often called a townhome community, but most homes are recorded as condominiums in the MLS. Ask about the specific floor plan and what the association insures before you make an offer.

### How big are the homes at The Heights?

1-bedrooms are about 770–800 sq ft, 2-bedrooms about 1,145–1,244 sq ft, and 3-bedrooms about 1,130–1,444 sq ft.

### Is The Heights a gated community?

Yes. The Heights has a controlled-access gated entrance.

### How quickly do homes at The Heights sell?

About 6 homes a year have sold here over the last 5 years, with a median of about 3 weeks on the market.

### Which schools serve The Heights?

Most listings show Carmel Del Mar Elementary (Del Mar Union), Carmel Valley Middle School and Torrey Pines High School (San Dieguito Union); some show Carmel Creek Elementary instead. Canyon Crest Academy is a district-wide option. Confirm with each district for a specific address.
