About The Heights Community

The Heights at Carmel Valley is a gated townhome community known for its Spanish-Mediterranean architecture, mature palm landscaping, and quiet, well-maintained streets in the heart of the 92130 zip code.

The gated entrance and consistent tile-roof, stucco architecture give The Heights a distinct, cohesive feel compared to some of the area's more varied communities. Its central Carmel Valley location puts residents within easy reach of top-rated schools, shopping, and the I-5/SR-56 corridor.

As a Keller Williams Realty agent who has worked this community for 11+ years, I can walk you through current HOA details, recent comparable sales, and what to look for in a specific building or floor plan.

The Heights Townhome FAQs

Is The Heights a gated community?

Yes — The Heights at Carmel Valley has a controlled-access gated entrance, which is one of the main reasons buyers are drawn to the community.

What do The Heights HOA fees cover?

Most Carmel Valley gated townhome HOAs like The Heights cover exterior maintenance, common-area landscaping, gate/security upkeep, and master insurance on shared structures. Exact fees vary by unit size — reach out for current figures.

Where is The Heights located in Carmel Valley?

The Heights sits in central Carmel Valley, within the 92130 zip code, convenient to top-rated schools, shopping, and the I-5/SR-56 corridor.

Is The Heights a good investment?

The Heights' gated setting, central location, and consistent architecture have historically supported steady demand in the Carmel Valley townhome market.
