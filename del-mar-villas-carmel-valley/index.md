## Life at Del Mar Villas

Del Mar Villas was built in 1990 as an apartment community and became condominiums in 2004. That history explains two things buyers notice right away: grounds with more than 30 years of mature trees and lawn, and an amenity list larger than most Carmel Valley condo complexes at this price. Every home is a single-level flat with its own patio or balcony.

### Why buyers choose Del Mar Villas

- **Walk to school.** Carmel Del Mar Elementary and Carmel Valley Middle School are each about 0.3 miles away, and Torrey Pines High is about a mile.
- **Walk to everything else.** The recreation center and library, Piazza Carmel, One Paseo and Del Mar Highlands Town Center are all within a 12-minute walk.
- **Low monthly dues.** Dues of about $330–$370 a month are among the lowest of any Carmel Valley condo community, and they cover water and trash.

## Is Del Mar Villas the Right Community for You?

Who it suits best, and the honest tradeoffs to weigh before you buy.

### A great fit for

- **Families** — Two schools about a third of a mile away, at a price well below Carmel Valley single-family homes.
- **First-Time Buyers** — 1-bedrooms have sold for under $600K, with low monthly dues and big-community amenities.
- **Investors** — Steady rental demand from school-zone families and the SR-56 job corridor, and dues that cover water and trash.

### Tradeoffs to weigh

- **Smaller homes** — Plans run 638–950 sq ft, and there are no 3-bedrooms. For more room, look at nearby townhomes such as Elysian.
- **Covered parking, no garages** — Homes come with covered parking and storage, not private garages.
- **Interiors vary a lot** — Built in 1990 and converted in 2004, so some homes are fully redone and others are close to original. The price gap can reach $100K.
- **Mello-Roos** — A small special tax, about $366–$452 a year, on top of the property tax.

## Del Mar Villas Carmel Valley FAQs

### How much do Del Mar Villas Carmel Valley homes sell for?

Recent sales at Del Mar Villas ranged from about $593,000 to $700,000. By size: 1-bedrooms $593K–$599K (median $599K; 3 sales in the last 24 months) and 2-bedrooms $664K–$700K (median $684K; 4 sales in the last 24 months). Condition, floor level and location in the community explain most of the gap. Ask for a current comparison before you make an offer.

### What are the monthly dues at Del Mar Villas?

MLS listings show $330–$370 a month: 2025 sales listed $330–$365, and the 2026 listings show $370. The dues cover water, trash, landscaping, exterior and roof maintenance, and the pool, spa, sauna, fitness center, courts and clubhouse. Confirm the current amount for a specific home.

### Does Del Mar Villas have Mello-Roos?

Yes, a small one: about $366–$452 a year, or roughly $30–$38 a month. Few listings report it, so check the tax bill for the specific home. The base property tax rate is about 1.07% of the purchase price.

### Are Del Mar Villas homes condos or townhomes?

They're condominiums, not townhomes. Every home is a single-level flat on one floor, either a ground-floor or an upper-floor unit, with a private patio or balcony.

### How big are the homes at Del Mar Villas?

There are three floor plans: a 638 sq ft 1-bedroom, 1-bath, and two 2-bedroom, 2-bath plans of about 864 and 950 sq ft.

### What is parking like at Del Mar Villas?

Homes come with covered parking and a storage space. There are no private garages.

### How quickly do Del Mar Villas condos sell?

The median time on market over the last 5 years was about 20 days, so well-priced homes usually go under contract within three weeks.

### I own at Del Mar Villas. What affects my sale price most?

Condition. Because the buildings date from 1990, buyers compare finishes closely, and a fully updated home can sell for $100K or more above one in original condition. Floor plan and location within the grounds matter next. I can show you which recent sales are true comparisons for your home.
