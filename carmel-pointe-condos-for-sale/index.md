## Life at Carmel Pointe

A 200-unit condo community built by Western Pacific Housing in 2001–2002, set in a quiet pocket that's still a short walk from Del Mar Highlands Town Center and One Paseo. High ceilings, private patios or decks, and underground and garage parking set it apart from older Carmel Valley condos. See the full [Carmel Pointe community profile](https://www.carmelvalley.com/condos-for-sale/carmel-pointe/).

### Why buyers choose Carmel Pointe

- **Walk to shopping and dining.** Del Mar Highlands Town Center is about a 5-minute walk, with One Paseo just beyond.
- **Garage parking, not carports.** Underground and garage parking come with the home, which is rare among Carmel Valley condos of this age.
- **Resort-style amenities.** An oversized pool, hydrotherapy spa, fitness center, clubhouse and BBQ area, all HOA-maintained.

## Is Carmel Pointe the Right Community for You?

Who it suits best, and the honest tradeoffs to weigh before you buy.

### A great fit for

- **Relocating Professionals** — Garage parking, a full gym and walkable shopping, with SR-56 and I-5 close by.
- **First-Time Buyers** — A 92130 address and top-rated schools, with water, sewer and trash already in the HOA.
- **Investors** — Steady rental demand near Del Mar Highlands, and a utility-inclusive HOA keeps costs predictable.

### Tradeoffs to weigh

- **Shared garage, not a private one** — Parking is underground or in shared garage structures, not an attached private garage.
- **Compact floor plans** — Most homes are 1- and 2-bedrooms under 1,200 sq ft. 3-bedrooms are rare.
- **Few sales to compare** — Only about 5 homes a year sell here, so good units go fast and recent comps are thin.
- **Interiors vary** — Some units still have their original 2002 finishes. Ask what's been updated.

## Carmel Pointe Condo FAQs

### What is the price range for Carmel Pointe condos?

Over the last 5 years, Carmel Pointe condos sold between about $540,000 and $1,000,000: 1-bedrooms $540K–$689K, 2-bedrooms $725K–$890K, and 3-bedrooms $850K–$1M.

### What do Carmel Pointe HOA fees cover?

The HOA is about $350 per month. It covers the pool, spa, fitness center, clubhouse, landscaping and common areas, plus water, sewer, trash and hot water.

### Does Carmel Pointe have Mello-Roos?

No — Carmel Pointe has no Mello-Roos, and the property tax rate is about 1.1% of assessed value.

### How big are the units at Carmel Pointe?

From about 704 sq ft (1-bedroom) to 1,387 sq ft (3-bedroom). Most sales were 2-bedrooms between about 960 and 1,190 sq ft.

### What is parking like at Carmel Pointe?

Parking is underground and in garages rather than surface carports. Ask which space conveys with a specific unit.

### How quickly do Carmel Pointe condos sell?

About 5 homes sell here a year. Over the last 5 years the median time on market was about 5 weeks; well-priced, updated units often go faster.

### How does Carmel Pointe compare to other Carmel Valley condo communities?

Carmel Pointe (2001–2002) is newer than neighboring Arborlake (1986–1989) and The Lakes, has garage parking instead of carports, and its ~$350/mo HOA is lower while also covering utilities.
