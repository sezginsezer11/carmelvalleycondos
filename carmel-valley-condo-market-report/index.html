What It Means

Reading the Carmel Valley Condo Market

For Buyers

The median condo and townhome price has climbed from $942,500 in 2022 to $995,000 year to date in 2026 — a gain of roughly 5.6% over five years, far steadier than the swings seen in detached single-family homes over the same stretch. Days on market in 2026 sit at 32, up from the 16-19 day pace of 2022-2024, giving buyers a bit more room to evaluate HOA financials and unit condition before competing.

For Sellers

Price per square foot has held in the high-$700s to low-$800s range every year since 2022, with 2026 pricing right in line with 2025. Units are still selling close to original list price — 97.9% to 100% most years — which means overpricing, not a soft market, is the main thing costing sellers time on market. A well-priced, well-presented condo in an established Carmel Valley community is still moving in roughly a month or less.

Good to Know

Market Report FAQ

What is the median price for a condo or townhome in Carmel Valley?

The median sale price for Carmel Valley condos and townhomes in 92130 is $995,000 year to date in 2026, based on 98 closed sales, up from a median of $970,000 in 2025.

Why are condos and townhomes reported together?

Listing agents frequently enter a townhome as a condo in the MLS, or the reverse, so the two property types are reported together as "attached homes" to keep the year-over-year data consistent and accurate.

How fast are Carmel Valley condos selling right now?

Condos and townhomes averaged 32 days on market in 2026 year to date, up slightly from 2024's 19-day average, but still selling at 97.9% of original list price — a sign that well-priced units continue to move efficiently.

Is now a good time to buy or sell a Carmel Valley condo?

Prices have risen steadily and days on market remain reasonable by historical standards, which tends to favor sellers who price accurately and buyers who are prepared to move quickly on well-presented units.
