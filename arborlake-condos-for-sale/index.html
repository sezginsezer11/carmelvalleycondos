About the Arborlake Community

Arborlake is a 190-unit condo community in southwest Carmel Valley, built by Baldwin Homes between 1986 and 1989. What sets it apart from newer 92130 developments is its water-feature setting: a central lake, multiple fountains and waterfalls, and paved walking paths winding through greenbelts that have had nearly four decades to mature.

Units range from 742-square-foot one-bedrooms to 1,145-square-foot two-bedroom layouts, with most patios and balconies oriented toward the lake and landscaping rather than the parking areas. The community sits just north of Carmel View Road and Camino Mira Del Mar — one of the earliest-developed pockets of Carmel Valley, so the surrounding sidewalks, parks, and street trees are as established as the grounds themselves.

As a Keller Williams Realty agent who has worked this community for 11+ years, I can walk you through current HOA details, recent comparable sales, and what to look for in a specific building or floor plan. What Stands Out

Three Reasons Buyers Choose Arborlake

A central lake with multiple fountains and waterfalls runs through the community — a setting no newer, denser 92130 development has room to replicate. Most patios and balconies face the water and landscaping rather than parking.

Resort-Style Amenities

Two lighted tennis courts, a heated pool, two spas, a dry sauna, and a fitness center — all HOA-maintained. Few condo communities this age in Carmel Valley still offer this full a recreation package.

Mature, Walkable Grounds

Built out between 1986 and 1989, Arborlake's greenbelts and paved walking paths have had nearly four decades to mature — a level of tree canopy and established landscaping that a newly built community simply can't offer yet.

Before You Buy

What to Know Before Buying at Arborlake

The honest tradeoffs, not just the highlights — so there are no surprises at inspection.

Carport parking, not a garage

Covered carport parking with storage is included, but it's not an enclosed garage — worth factoring in if that matters for your lifestyle or a second vehicle.

Floor plans vary by building

Some units are ground-floor walk-ups, others are upper-level with stairs — confirm the specific floor, elevator access, and layout before falling in love with a listing photo.

HOA covers the exterior, not the interior

The ~$408/mo HOA handles building exteriors, the lake, landscaping, and amenities. Interior updates — kitchens, baths, flooring — are the owner's responsibility, so ask about a specific unit's renovation history.

No private yard

Like any condo community, outdoor space is a patio or balcony, not a yard. If private outdoor space is a priority, compare unit size and patio orientation carefully — lake-facing units are the most sought after.

Visiting in Person

What to Check When You Tour Arborlake

Five things worth inspecting or asking about on a showing — bring this list.

  • 1

    Which way the unit faces

    Ask whether the patio or balcony faces the lake and landscaping, or the parking and carports — it's the single biggest value driver within the community.

  • 2

    Floor level and stairs

    Confirm whether it's a ground-floor walk-up or an upper unit with stairs, and ask about elevator access for that specific building — it matters for daily life and resale.

  • 3

    Noise from the amenities

    Units near the tennis courts or pool can pick up activity noise on weekends — worth a visit at a busy time if that matters to you.

  • 4

    Age of interior systems

    The buildings date to 1986–1989. Ask the seller's agent for the age of the water heater, HVAC, and any plumbing updates specific to that unit.

  • 5

    Assigned carport and storage

    Confirm which carport space and storage unit convey with the home, and how close they are to the actual front door — this varies by building.

Good to Know

Arborlake Condo FAQs

What is the price range for Arborlake condos?

Arborlake condos typically sell between $650,000 and $875,000, or roughly $680–$820 per square foot depending on the unit's condition and position within the community.

What do Arborlake HOA fees cover?

Arborlake's HOA runs approximately $408 per month and covers the community's central lake and fountain system, two lighted tennis courts, a heated pool, two spas, a dry sauna, a fitness center, all greenbelt landscaping, exterior building maintenance, and covered carport parking with storage.

Does Arborlake have Mello-Roos?

No — Arborlake carries no Mello-Roos assessment, with a property tax rate of approximately 1.07% of assessed value.

How big are the units at Arborlake?

Arborlake units range from 742-square-foot one-bedrooms to 1,145-square-foot two-bedroom layouts.

How does Arborlake compare to other Carmel Valley condo communities?

Arborlake's HOA runs higher than comparable communities like The Lakes (~$397/mo), Carmel Pointe (~$350/mo), and Del Mar Villas (~$340/mo) — the difference mostly funds the central lake, fountains, and waterfalls that those communities don't have.

How quickly do Arborlake condos sell?

Well-priced, updated listings typically go under contract within 2–3 weeks, reflecting a buyer pool that often targets this specific community rather than comparison-shopping broadly.
