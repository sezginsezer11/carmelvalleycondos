How it's organized

About this map
  • Categories are the 5 folders in your My Maps file. Areas group the condo and townhome communities by location. Their names are compass directions, so rename them however you describe these areas to clients.
  • Distances are the shortest gap between two boundaries. "Touching" means the boundaries meet.
  • Repeated names: several communities are drawn as more than one shape (Heights ×3, Belmont ×3, and pairs for Alta Mar, Promontory, San Remo, Santa Fe Summit, Sonoma, Steeplechase, Lexington, Derby Hill, Costa Del Sol and Brightwater). Each is shown here as one community.
  • Worth checking in My Maps: one shape is still named "Polygon 75", "4x4 Construction" looks like a placeholder name, and "Bridleridge" and "Bridle Ridge" may be the same community.
