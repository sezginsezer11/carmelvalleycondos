## Life at The Lakes

Built by Baldwin Homes in 1984–1985, The Lakes is set around five small lakes connected by streams, with koi and lily ponds, grassy walkways and four decades of mature trees. It's one of the most affordable ways into a 92130 address, with no Mello-Roos. See the full [The Lakes community profile](https://www.carmelvalley.com/condos-for-sale/the-lakes/).

### Why buyers choose The Lakes

- **Water everywhere you look.** Lakes, streams and koi ponds run through the grounds, and many units look out on them.
- **Walk to errands and schools.** Piazza Carmel is about 5 minutes on foot, and the elementary and middle schools are within half a mile.
- **No Mello-Roos.** Older construction means no special tax on top of the property tax — a real monthly saving next to newer communities.

## Is The Lakes the Right Community for You?

Who it suits best, and the honest tradeoffs to weigh before you buy.

### A great fit for

- **First-Time Buyers** — One of the lowest price points in 92130, with no Mello-Roos and top-rated schools within walking distance.
- **Downsizers** — Single-level living, HOA-managed grounds, and dinner at Piazza Carmel or One Paseo without driving.
- **Investors** — Steady rental demand from the SR-56 and Sorrento Valley job centers, and no Mello-Roos in the numbers.

### Tradeoffs to weigh

- **Carport parking, no garages** — Each unit has a reserved covered carport space with storage, but there are no garages.
- **Higher HOA than nearby condos** — About $599/mo in 2026, more than Arborlake or Carmel Pointe. Ask what reserves and recent projects look like.
- **1980s buildings** — Built in 1984–1985; interiors range from original to fully updated, so compare finishes carefully.
- **Few 3-bedrooms** — Almost every sale is a 1- or 2-bedroom; for more space, look at Chateau Village or The Groves next door.

## The Lakes Condo FAQs

### What is the price range for The Lakes condos?

Over the last 5 years, The Lakes condos sold between about $550,000 and $935,000: 1-bedrooms $550K–$815K (median $644K) and 2-bedrooms $659K–$935K (median $805K).

### What are the HOA fees at The Lakes?

MLS listings show $560–$599 per month: 2025 sales listed $560–$565, and every 2026 listing shows $599. The fee covers the pool, spa, tennis and pickleball courts, the lakes and koi ponds, landscaping, exterior maintenance and the community room. Confirm the current fee for a specific unit.

### Does The Lakes have Mello-Roos?

No. None of the recent MLS listings at The Lakes show a Mello-Roos assessment, and the property tax rate is about 1.07% of assessed value.

### How big are the units at The Lakes?

From about 791 sq ft (1-bedroom) to 1,269 sq ft (2-bedroom). Most 1-bedrooms are 790–950 sq ft and most 2-bedrooms are 1,100–1,270 sq ft.

### What is parking like at The Lakes?

Each unit has a reserved covered carport space with extra storage, plus guest parking and EV charging stations. There are no garages.

### How quickly do The Lakes condos sell?

About 8 homes a year have sold here over the last 5 years. The median time on market was about 12 days, so well-priced units go quickly.

### How does The Lakes compare to other Carmel Valley condo communities?

The Lakes and neighboring Arborlake are the two Carmel Valley condo communities built around water. The Lakes is a few years older, sells at similar prices, has a higher HOA ($560–$599 vs. $450–$460) and carport parking. Carmel Pointe is newer with garage parking and a lower HOA.
