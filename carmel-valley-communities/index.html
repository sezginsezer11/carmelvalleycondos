Carmel Valley Communities: Common Questions

How many condo and townhome communities are in Carmel Valley?

This map shows 41 condo and townhome communities in Carmel Valley (San Diego, 92130), with more than 5,700 homes between them. They are spread across 5 areas, and 23 of them are south of Del Mar Heights Road.

Which part of Carmel Valley has the most condos?

South of Del Mar Heights Road. It has 23 of the 41 condo and townhome communities on this map, so it usually has the most listings and the widest range of sizes and prices.

What are the largest condo communities in Carmel Valley?

By number of homes, the largest are Pell Place (317 homes), Camino Villas (316 homes), East Bluff (306 homes). Bigger communities tend to have more sales each year, which makes prices easier to compare.

Are there newer condos and townhomes in Carmel Valley?

Yes. Pacific Highlands Ranch, in the east of Carmel Valley, was built out from the 2000s onward, so its attached homes are newer. Many communities there carry a Mello-Roos tax on top of the HOA fee, so compare the full monthly cost.

How do I find out what is for sale in a specific community?

Tap the community on the map or its card below. Where we have a community guide, it shows live listings, recent sales, HOA details and schools. For any other community, call or email Sez Sezer for current listings and recent sales.
