How to Choose a Carmel Valley Condo Community

Start with location, then compare what you pay each month and what you get for it. Two homes at the same price can cost very different amounts to own once monthly dues and Mello-Roos are added, and a community’s age, size and layout shape day-to-day life more than its name does.

Area and commute

Communities south of Del Mar Heights Road are closest to the shops, offices and I-5/SR-56. Pacific Highlands Ranch is newer and further east; Torrey Hills sits near Sorrento Valley.

Condo or townhome

Condos are often single-level with shared walls above or below. Townhomes usually have two or more levels, an attached garage and no one overhead.

Full monthly cost

Add the monthly dues, any Mello-Roos tax and property tax to the mortgage. Newer communities often carry Mello-Roos; older ones may have lower dues but older systems.

Schools

Most of Carmel Valley is in the Del Mar Union and San Dieguito Union districts, but school assignment depends on the address. Each community guide lists its schools.

Size and turnover

Larger communities have more sales each year, so prices are easier to compare and there is more to choose from. Smaller ones come up less often.

Rules and amenities

Pools, gyms, pet rules and rental limits differ by community. Ask for the HOA documents before you commit, and check the reserves.

Carmel Valley Communities: Common Questions

How many condo and townhome communities are in Carmel Valley?

This map shows 41 condo and townhome communities in Carmel Valley (San Diego, 92130), with more than 5,700 homes between them. They are spread across 5 areas, and 23 of them are south of Del Mar Heights Road.

Which part of Carmel Valley has the most condos?

South of Del Mar Heights Road. It has 23 of the 41 condo and townhome communities on this map, so it usually has the most listings and the widest range of sizes and prices.

What are the largest condo communities in Carmel Valley?

By number of homes, the largest are Pell Place (317 homes), Camino Villas (316 homes), East Bluff (306 homes). Bigger communities tend to have more sales each year, which makes prices easier to compare.

Are there newer condos and townhomes in Carmel Valley?

Yes. Pacific Highlands Ranch, in the east of Carmel Valley, was built out from the 2000s onward, so its attached homes are newer. Many communities there carry a Mello-Roos tax on top of the HOA fee, so compare the full monthly cost.

Is there a list of every condo community in Carmel Valley?

Yes. The list beside the map on this page has all 41 condo and townhome communities in Carmel Valley, grouped by area, with their type and number of homes. Open an area, then tap a community to see it on the map.

How do I find out what is for sale in a specific community?

Tap the community on the map, then See homes. Where we have a community guide, it shows live listings, recent sales, monthly dues and schools. For any other community, call or email Sez Sezer for current listings and recent sales.
