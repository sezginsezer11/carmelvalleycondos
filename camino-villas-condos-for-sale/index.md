## Life at Camino Villas

Built in 1993–1997, Camino Villas is one of Carmel Valley's larger condo communities: 316 homes along two quiet loop streets, set among mature trees and lush common grounds. Nearly every home is a 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo of about 950 sq ft, which makes it one of the more affordable ways into a 92130 address. See the full [Camino Villas community profile](https://www.carmelvalley.com/condos-for-sale/camino-villas/).

### Why buyers choose Camino Villas

- **Resort-style grounds.** The pool, spa and tennis courts sit among landscaping that has had 30 years to grow in.
- **Walk to the center of Carmel Valley.** The recreation center is about 8 minutes on foot, and One Paseo, Piazza Carmel and Del Mar Highlands are all within about 15 minutes.
- **Easy to compare.** With dozens of near-identical 2-bedroom sales, it's easy to see what a unit is worth and whether a list price is fair.

## Is Camino Villas the Right Community for You?

Who it suits best, and the honest tradeoffs to weigh before you buy.

### A great fit for

- **First-Time Buyers** — A 2-bedroom, 2-bath in 92130 at one of the area's lower price points, with top-rated schools nearby.
- **Downsizers** — Single-level condo living, HOA-managed grounds, and the pool and spa without the upkeep.
- **Investors** — Uniform 2-bedroom floor plans make rents and resale easy to predict, close to the Sorrento Valley and SR-56 job centers.

### Tradeoffs to weigh

- **Mello-Roos on top of property tax** — Most listings show about $366–$540 a year. Ask for the exact amount on a unit's tax bill.
- **Carport parking, not garages** — Listings show assigned carport spaces; there are no private garages.
- **Few 1-bedrooms** — Only about one 1-bedroom sells a year, so options at the lowest price point are limited.
- **1990s buildings** — Interiors range from original to fully updated, so compare finishes carefully.

## Camino Villas Condo FAQs

### What do Camino Villas condos sell for?

Recent sales at Camino Villas ranged from about $500,000 to $775,000. By size: 1-bedrooms $500K–$565K (median $510K; 3 sales in the last 24 months) and 2-bedrooms $719K–$775K (median $750K; 5 sales in the last 12 months). Condition, floor level and location in the community explain most of the gap. Ask for a current comparison before you make an offer.

### What are the monthly dues at Camino Villas?

MLS listings show $375–$450 a month. Most 2025 sales listed $420, and 2026 listings show $420–$450. The fee covers the pool, spa, tennis courts, fitness room, landscaping and exterior maintenance. Confirm the current fee for a specific unit.

### Does Camino Villas have Mello-Roos?

Yes. Most recent listings show a Mello-Roos (Community Facilities District) assessment of about $366–$540 a year on top of the regular property tax. Ask for the exact figure on a unit's tax bill.

### How big are the condos at Camino Villas?

1-bedroom, 1-bath condos are about 624 sq ft, and 2-bedroom, 2-bath condos are about 944–974 sq ft. Over the last 5 years, 41 of 47 sales were 2-bedrooms.

### What amenities does Camino Villas have?

A pool and spa, tennis courts, a fitness room and mature, resort-style landscaping. Listings also show a sauna, assigned carport parking and guest parking.

### How quickly do Camino Villas condos sell?

About 9 homes a year have sold here over the last 5 years, with a median of about 17 days on the market.

### Which schools serve Camino Villas?

Listings show Carmel Del Mar Elementary (Del Mar Union), Carmel Valley Middle School and Torrey Pines High School (San Dieguito Union). Canyon Crest Academy is a district-wide option. School assignments can change, so confirm with each district for a specific address.

### How does Camino Villas compare to Arborlake next door?

Arborlake is older (1986–1989), built around a lake, with higher dues ($450–$460) and no Mello-Roos. Camino Villas is newer and larger, with mostly 2-bedroom, 2-bath condos and lower dues, but a small Mello-Roos assessment.
